Upper Camping Pad

Your own mountaintop.
No neighbours for miles.

The pad sits right at the peak of the mountain. Pitch a tent or park your RV, then walk the 62 private acres or head straight into 900+ acres of public land that connects through the property. Watch deer at dawn, and stars at night.

  • Completely privateOne site on the whole property. No other campers on your 62 acres.
  • Up to 10 guests · 2 vehiclesBring the whole crew. Picnic table and 6 chairs are set up for you.
  • Private fire pitBring your own firewood. Fires are off during Douglas County burn bans.
  • Dogs welcomeOn or off leash. Lots of guests bring their pups.
  • 4WD recommendedDirt road to the top. Your host sends clear directions and road videos after booking.
